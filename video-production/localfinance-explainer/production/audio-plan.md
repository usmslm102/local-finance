Segment files are synthesized locally; no identity imitation or cloud service. Source text exactly matches script.md. Pronounce LocalFinance as 'Local Finance'; PDF/CSV as letters; Excel as usual. Acronym expansion can affect measured durations.
Music: original synthesized warm sine/triangle pad in D major, sparse plucked arpeggio, 96 BPM. Low level beneath voice, gentle intro and final fade. No sampled third-party recording. SFX: original soft paper ticks/data pings at chapter changes and file import confirmation. No loud whooshes or impact hits.
Voice clips begin 12 frames into each scene. Actual voice duration is measured before final timeline lock; keep at least 0.3 seconds after speech before next scene. Caption phrases are allocated across measured voiced windows, manually checked against scene meaning; no claim of word-level forced alignment. Burned-in captions plus matching SRT/WebVTT.
Mix voice primary, bed approx -30 dBFS, pings below voice; final stereo mix normalized to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP and measured with ffmpeg. Full film exactly 1800 frames. No speech under the final brand hold after final line.
