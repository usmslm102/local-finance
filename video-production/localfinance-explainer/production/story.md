# Story
Logline: Scattered financial statements become a clear, personal ledger that stays on your computer.
Hook: Where does your money actually go? Setup: meet the offline workspace. Middle: import supported statements, review and categorize records, read spending and budget insights. Resolution: show the local architecture, then hold the brand and download cue.
The recurring visual is a ledger line: paper rows become record rows, category bars, and local storage layers. The story teaches cause and effect rather than listing every feature. The takeaway is Import → Review → Understand. Scope intentionally excludes investments and AI integrations to fit a readable one-minute introduction.
