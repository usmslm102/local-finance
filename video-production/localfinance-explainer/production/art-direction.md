# Art direction
Original motion graphics derived from docs/styles.css and DESIGN.md. Ink #07110d, paper #f4f7f3, mint #69e7ad, emerald #16b978. Bundled Inter variable; tabular INR numbers. Headlines 88–112px, core labels 38–48px, captions 39px. Primary content stays within x=88…992, y=120…1620; captions y=1660…1790. Small step/illustration labels are secondary.
Alternating dark and paper chapters, crisp ledger rules, oversized figures, soft layered shadows. No stock footage or decorative AI imagery. Existing shield mark is the only logo. Typography leads; one focal object moves at a time. Ease-out entries, staggered rows, restrained push-ins, direct chapter cuts with consistent fixed brand footer. Meaningful animation completes early enough for a readable hold.
Keyframes: scattered paper on ink; large shield within workstation; file into preview; merchant row to category; budget fill under spending bars; architecture enclosed by computer; mint final brand hold. These are stylized explanatory graphics, with persistent 'Illustrative demo · Fictional data' labeling during the demonstration.
