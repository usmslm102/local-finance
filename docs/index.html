Set up MCP ↗

Off by default. You control access.

Enable or disable MCP in Settings → AI / MCP. Connect your tool using the provided configuration and an access token. Rotate the token to revoke existing client access.

Read-only financial context.

AI tools can retrieve insights and transaction details. They cannot edit your ledger, import statements, or change your settings. Keep LocalFinance running while you use the connection.

Choose your AI tool with care.

LocalFinance serves data locally and never contacts an AI provider. Your connected AI tool may send retrieved financial data to its model provider. Its privacy settings and policies apply.

Built-in updates.
You stay in control.

LocalFinance checks public GitHub Releases in the background, with results cached for four hours. When a release is available, review the release notes in the app. Supported standalone installations offer Update & Restart; macOS app installations offer Download Release.

Prefer to opt out? Turn off Automatic Update Checks in Settings. You can still choose Check for Updates whenever you want.

Your financial data stays local.

Release checks and update downloads contact GitHub. Your statements, transactions, and database stay on your machine.

macOS app installations offer a complete DMG download to preserve the app signature. Quit LocalFinance before replacing the app in Applications; your local database is preserved. Standalone executables support Update & Restart when their installation is writable.
